## Helpful Details for Bug Reports

Including a few details upfront helps us diagnose issues much faster:

- Your operating system (macOS / Linux / Windows) and Python version (`python --version`)
- Your Annolid version (`annolid --version`) and install profile (`gui`, `tracking`, `workstation`, etc.)
- A minimal reproducible example (a short 5-second video clip, sample JSON, or exact `annolid-run` command)
- The full error message or terminal traceback
